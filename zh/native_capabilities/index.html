    已安装的原生能力声明¶

    当前为 31 个模块、38 类部件、51 种操作、27 种仪器。下表由运行时注册表生成， 保留英文合同原文以精确呈现每个模型的范围；中文领域概览见扩展范围。 实际动作还受组装、设备与任务约束，模块声明不保证任意跨域耦合。

    Module Components / strategies Scope
    aqueous_gas_transfer aqueous_gas_transfer, finite-aqueous-gas-transfer Finite ideal-gas headspace and ideal aqueous liquid in one rigid isothermal vessel; reversible sourced gas/liquid transfer and fast aqueous reactions, actual gas pressure and same-reference internal-energy bath duty; optional seeded pure-mineral kinetics with actual solid volume displacing the headspace. Nonvolatile solvent, fixed liquid volume and zero solute partial volumes; no infinite gas source, pressure flow or spontaneous mineral nucleation.
    aqueous_mineral aqueous_mineral, finite-carbonate-calcite-equilibrium Authored finite aqueous acid/base/complexation and mineral equilibrium; explicit thermodynamic data and ionic-strength domain, not a geochemical database. Also finite same-T/P solution mixing, nonideal equilibrium precipitation and dilution with liquid-only packets, original solid inventory, same-reference enthalpy and pressure-volume work; instantaneous equilibrium, not nucleation kinetics.
    aqueous_reactive_transport aqueous_reactive_transport, finite-aqueous-reactive-transport Sealed one-dimensional ideal-dilute aqueous transport at fixed T/P; finite stationary water, zero solute partial volumes, zero-current electrodiffusion, fast equilibrium and declared slow neutral conversion. Also finite feed/collector throughflow with conservative upwind advection, common mechanical dispersion and actual water/solute/enthalpy transfer; prescribed kinematic flow at common T/P, without a pump model. Strictly positive ions; no pressure flow, mineral or external circuit.
    battery_spm battery_spm, battery_cell, single-particle-battery Synthetic SPM with calorics/SEI, or explicit isothermal SPMe-v1 with conservative electrolyte transport; shared native cycling. Explicit isothermal DFN has coupled spatial reactions and a fixed synthetic qualification at 24 through-plane cells per region / 64 radial cells. Explicit isothermal Newman-Tobias uses two average lithium inventories and spatial porous electrical fields, without radial diffusion or dynamic salt; its fixed CV grid-inventory qualification remains open. Development MPM adds shared-potential size populations with independent inventories; independent MPM scientific qualification remains pending. Isothermal single-material SPM/MPM may declare current-sigmoid OCP or one persistent Axen hysteresis coordinate per electrode; no hysteresis thermal/SEI coupling or equilibrium-SOH interpretation. Through-thickness thermal SPM uses three finite layers and two collectors, constant net OCV and local BV temperature feedback with ambient cooling; no arbitrary external thermal device or general material thermal claim. Isothermal MSMR-SPM uses one or two explicit reactions per electrode with potential-driven radial diffusion and shared branch kinetics; no MSMR thermal, EIS or automatic equilibrium-SOH claim. Composite SPM supports two independent negative material phases and one positive phase with single-valued monotone OCV, including internal exchange at zero terminal current; no thermal/SEI, EIS, SOH or energy closure. Positive-working half-cell SPM uses finite lithium metal with explicitly fixed geometry; no half-cell thermal/SEI, EIS, SOH or energy closure. Coarser meshes and other parameters need convergence checks; Closed volume-consistent isothermal lead-acid LOQS uses six real chemical inventories and the same runtime; no heat, side reactions, EIS or automatic SOH. Synthetic parameters are not wet-lab validation. Split OCV-R uses two finite mean Li inventories, separate electrode OCV and capacity, and series resistance; no particle diffusion, BV or heat. Its EIS and equilibrium SOH reuse the same declared inventory model. No PyBaMM runtime backend.
    battery_thevenin battery_thevenin, thevenin-empirical-circuit Sourced empirical RC and two-node cell/jig heat model; shared CC/CV control and persistent apparatus. No chemical inventory/total energy, diffusion element or independent released-model qualification yet.
    continuous_flow continuous_flow See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    core material_input, sample_storage, reaction See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    crystallization aqueous_filter, crystal_thermal, crystallization, finite-crystal-population-temperature Authored crystal seeding, cooling and wet-cake operations; also declared same-T/P instantaneous aqueous mineral filtration into two finite empty receivers, with explicit solid capture and mother-liquid retention, actual leaked solids, wet-cake washing and repeat separation. Database filtration has no drying, pressure-drop or filtration-rate model.
    distillation distillation See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    dynamic_distillation dynamic_distillation, finite-equilibrium-column Finite tray/receiver inventories, heat and timed cuts; authored small dynamic column, not arbitrary plant design.
    electrical_control transient_electrode, finite-transient-electrode See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    electrochemistry electrochemistry See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    finite_transport finite_transport, finite-pump-transport Finite pump, pipe, tanks and authored heat exchange; bounded working point; or four finite tanks and two pumps exchanging countercurrent heat at declared UA with independent circuit flows; or declared isothermal zero-order liquid treatment chains with finite product/concentrate stocks and receipt-based engineering economics; or finite pump-fed neutral reactive holdup with ideal overflow to a finite product tank, volume-compatible chemistry and one shared bath; or finite neutral aqueous mixed-contact adsorption and rinse with persistent competitive loading, five liquid stocks and one shared bath; or finite ideal-dilute Na/Ca resin exchange with counterion release, salt regeneration and rinse across six liquid stocks, weighted fixed resin charge and one shared bath; or finite neutral suspensions with a declared jar-test endpoint, explicit dispersed/floc particle phases, empirical clarification into actual clear-water/wet-sludge tanks, and mixing/pump work; no additive-driven coagulation prediction or settling dynamics; the adsorption-world feed also supports read-only single-solute fresh GAC design and breakthrough queries at fixed influent, using actual pump flow and feed volume above pickup; not remaining-life prediction for existing LDF media. These are declared finite models, not arbitrary multiphase process-network simulation.
    gas_reactor_network gas_reactor_network, finite-isothermal-gas-network Finite isothermal ideal-gas reactors, storage and collection nodes; declared molar/mass flow and one-way pressure valves; shared time, material transfers and bath heat; or explicit per-node energy with finite vessel heat capacities, source-temperature enthalpy transport, environment/neighbor heat exchange and one queued anchor heater. Complete imported ideal surfaces can share the same gas network, owned site stocks and calorics. Also sealed or finite-feed/collector isothermal low-Mach planar cells with common grid pressure, independent tank pressures, continuity-derived bulk flow and whole-mixture diffusion; prescribed boundary molar flow has no momentum or compressor model. Alternatively one constant-load finite-stroke ideal-gas piston with actual EOS volume and separately booked pressure work, without surfaces or spatial cells. Constant-volume energy networks can instead declare one finite-heat-capacity wall with its own persistent temperature, gas contact conduction and grey radiation/conduction to an environment. No wall/piston, wall/surface or wall/spatial coupling; no piston inertia, spatial flame field or arbitrary member heat queues. Sourced finite harmonic state counting can compile one-well RRKM channels into the original energy-grain populations and bath collisions, with explicit ZPE and quantization; no rotor, tunneling or general pressure-rate reduction. Read-only single-well QSD rates, product branches and spectral gaps use current original grains and actual bath inventory or explicit fixed T/bath-pressure grids; no dynamic reduction, total-pressure PLog or new heat/inventory model.
    lab_schedule lab_schedule Single process; finite independent closed systems with explicitly declared shared-clock strategies and atomic finite pump/membrane paths; explicit finite heater/analyzer identities with FIFO occupancy; no same-vessel hybrid or general loops.
    liquid_equilibrium liquid_equilibrium, isothermal-liquid-equilibrium Strict finite liquid phase equilibrium using declared component properties and supported composition domain; no silent extrapolation.
    mineral_kinetics mineral_kinetics, finite-calcite-kinetics Finite calcite dissolution/precipitation with fast aqueous equilibrium; fixed 298.15 K; or declared database minerals at fixed TP with fast aqueous reactions, finite seeded surface and same-reference heat/work. Database mode currently permits wait only; no mineral nucleation.
    molecular_process molecular_process, molecular-process-receiver Declared external source through homogeneous serial units or one recycle loop into one finite receiver; wait and snapshots; local clock only.
    observation observation See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    phase phase See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    programmed_chromatography programmed_chromatography Authored peak/retention response, finite sampling and persistent carryover; not a fitted universal chromatographic transport model.
    ro_membrane ro_membrane, finite-dilute-ro Finite ideal dilute NaCl RO at 298.15 K with deposited foulant/rinse, or declared binary-electrolyte axial RO with pressure loss, same-source aqueous calorics and three finite tanks; or ideal dilute multi-ion Donnan/steric/Born NF with a resolved pore and a mixed-feed 0D module; or a periodic internal AEM/CEM electrodialysis pair with two finite liquids, signed ion/water transfer and bounded CC/CV control; or two-tank direct-contact Knudsen membrane distillation with finite heat/cooling and signed water/energy transfer; model-specific T/P/salinity bounds and operations, no membrane holdup or full WaterTAP flowsheet.
    sbml sbml_model, formal-sbml-shared Formal 3D SBML quantity coordinates and supported transverse events; no declared chemical formula, carrier mass or heat model. Read-only finite-time perturbation growth for smooth autonomous reaction ODEs uses an explicit mol metric and the original stoichiometric subspace; no event/rate-rule tangents or asymptotic chaos claim.
    separation separation See the authored physical spec and runtime contract; no universal validity claim.
    solid_solution solid_solution, calphad_phase, binary-solid-equilibrium Finite binary equilibrium, selected native TDB/CEF phase, or declared multiphase CEF PH equilibrium with latent heat and finite thermal device; finite phase and tangent search, without global uniqueness claims. Fixed-T/P neutral vacancy-free ternary CEF sections retain requested composition nodes, tie lines/triangles and failed points; read-only world queries solve the exact current elemental composition, without interpolating continuous boundaries or replacing live inventory.
    sorbent_contact sorbent_contact Competitive finite sorbent kinetics, owned carrier and finite wash; explicit condensed calorics and temperature domain.
    stochastic stochastic, gillespie-direct Integer Gillespie direct or explicit Gibson-Bruck next-reaction; constant volume/temperature mass action, persistent channel clocks and RNG; or declared fixed-partition continuous/stochastic feedback with integrated hazards, no cross-partition net conversion and no consuming sampling; not spatial kMC, tau-leaping or automatic ODE/SSA switching.
    surface_kinetics surface_reaction Finite owned sites; declared single/multisite mean-field mechanisms and symmetric interactions; not spatial kMC or a real catalyst fit.
    thermal thermal, heater_connection, owned-inventory-temperature, static-finite-carrier-stock, legacy-liquid-enthalpy Finite-power or explicitly isothermal device; shared energy accounting; manual reconnection requires one disabled heater in local-clock mode.
    vacuum_concentration vacuum_concentration, finite-vacuum-concentration-enthalpy Finite variable-composition concentration with authored VLE/calorics; unsupported kinetic coupling is rejected.
    vapor_liquid_equilibrium vapor_liquid_equilibrium, constant-pressure-flash-enthalpy Finite TP/PH closure with explicit phase/caloric models, including simultaneous two-phase molecular PH on a declared local branch with finite thermal devices and explicit constant-pressure boundary work; no simultaneous undeclared kinetic process.
